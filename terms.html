Parts Department Terms & Warranty

This section constitutes an agreement between the customer and Victoria Used Parts regarding the purchase of used auto parts.

1. Warranty Coverage

Standard Warranty: 3 Months (Parts Only) unless otherwise stated on your invoice.

Warranty covers the engine block, cylinder head, and internal components (for engines) and the case and internal components (for transmissions). Warranty is void if the assembly is dismantled.

2. Exclusions (What is NOT covered)

  • Labor costs, towing, loss of income, or downtime.
  • Consumables (gaskets, filters, spark plugs, belts, water pumps, oil seals).
  • Oil leaks of any kind (it is the fitter's responsibility to replace seals).
  • Damage caused by overheating, lack of maintenance, or improper installation.
  • Accessories attached to engines (turbochargers, alternators, starter motors, compressors) are not covered unless specified.
  • Timing belt failure (belts must be replaced prior to installation).

3. Installation Requirements

To maintain your warranty, parts must be installed by a licensed mechanic. You must:

  • Replace all oils, fluids, and filters.
  • Check and replace the timing belt/chain if necessary.
  • Service the cooling system (radiator, thermostat, hoses).
  • Replace oil seals (crank, cam, rear main) to prevent leaks.

4. Returns & Refunds

Change of Mind: We do not offer refunds for change of mind or incorrect choice. Please ensure you have ordered the correct part for your vehicle make and model.

Restocking Fee: If a return is approved at our discretion, a 20% restocking fee applies. Parts must be returned in the same condition as sold.

Defective Parts: If a part is faulty within the warranty period, we will offer a replacement. If no replacement is available, we will issue a store credit or refund.

Please retain your invoice. No warranty claims will be processed without a valid tax invoice.

Disputes: If you have any issues with a part or service, please contact us at sales@victoriausedparts.com.au before leaving a review so we can resolve the issue promptly.
