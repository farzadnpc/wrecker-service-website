Melbourne auto wreckers with nationwide reach

Victoria Used Parts helps mechanics, panel beaters and DIY car enthusiasts access reliable second-hand parts without the guesswork. Every component we stock is dismantled in-house, inspected by technicians and backed by a real support team.

Recycled parts that perform

From late-model hatchbacks to 4x4s and commercial vehicles, we salvage low-kilometre donors and test key assemblies before they are catalogued. That means less time troubleshooting and more time getting vehicles back on the road.

Transparent sourcing & reporting

Receive clear photos, condition grading and VIN matching so you can confidently quote customers. Workshop accounts benefit from priority alerts when the makes you rely on arrive in the yard.

Eco-conscious auto recycling

Re-using good components keeps tonnes of material out of landfill and cuts the energy required to manufacture new parts. Choosing recycled spares supports a circular economy for motoring in Victoria.
